YUXI ZHONG

Materiality, Ritual, Visuality, Vernacular, Tibet

Hi, I'm Yuxi Zhong. You can also call me Sonam Dorji!

I study how objects, materials, images, and acts of making shape religious life, social memory, and people’s relationships with place. My work is grounded in long-term fieldwork in Xiangcheng (乡城), my hometown on the eastern edge of the Tibetan Plateau. My research explores visual and material culture, vernacular religion, craft knowledge, and human-animal relations.

Beyond my academic work, I am also drawn to Peking Opera, photography, animal welfare, and cooking.
